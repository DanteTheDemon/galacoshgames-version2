Privacy Policy for No Safe Tile

Effective date: November 2, 2026  |  Provided by: Galacosh Games

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game No Safe Tile ("the app") collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or any account information. The app has no sign-in or user accounts.

The app and the third-party services it uses automatically collect the following technical data:

  • Device and advertising identifiers: the Android advertising ID and an app installation ID, plus device model and operating system version.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address by our advertising provider (no precise GPS location is accessed).
  • App activity: how you use the app, for example levels played, buttons used and game progress events.
  • Crash logs, diagnostics and performance data: technical information about errors and how well the app runs.

Your game progress, settings and in-game currency are stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • App functionality: to keep the game stable and fix crashes.
  • Analytics: to understand how the game is played and to improve levels and features.
  • Advertising: to show ads and measure their performance. Ads may be personalized based on your advertising ID.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, which collect data as described in their own policies:

Service Purpose Privacy policy
Google Firebase Analytics Usage analytics policies.google.com/privacy
Google Firebase Crashlytics Crash reporting and diagnostics policies.google.com/privacy
Google AdMob Advertising policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising Choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Doing so will not remove ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

5. In-App Purchases

The app is free to play and may include optional in-app purchases and rewarded ads. Purchases, if available, are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Refunds are handled according to Google Play's refund policy.

6. Data Retention

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for a limited period according to the retention settings we configure in Firebase, after which they are deleted or anonymized. Locally stored game data stays on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we process. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

No Safe Tile is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to opt out of personalized advertising. Because we hold no account data, we can usually only identify you through the app installation ID or advertising ID. To make a request, contact us at the email below and include, if possible, the relevant identifier. You can also delete locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:
